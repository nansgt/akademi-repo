Ghana administrative-area data attribution

The 16-region and 261-district names in ghana-admin-areas.json are adapted
from EricBidopa/ghana-geo-data, data/ghana-locations.json, commit
c4570a4ea96445f9c58f0796dd7b8f0c9934bd84.

Source repository: https://github.com/EricBidopa/ghana-geo-data

The source repository attributes its district structure to Ghana's Ministry
of Local Government district register. Confirm current administrative names
