Ghana SHS subject-name source

The searchable subject names are compiled from the Ministry of Education
curriculum microsite's SHS Year 1, Year 2 and Year 3 subject pages:

- https://curriculumresources.edu.gh/year1/
- https://curriculumresources.edu.gh/year2/
- https://curriculumresources.edu.gh/year3/

The lists include the union of subjects shown on those pages at the time of
compilation, plus common equivalent spellings for search. Schools may offer
different combinations, and teachers can type a subject that is not listed.
Confirm subject names and availability against the current Ministry source.
